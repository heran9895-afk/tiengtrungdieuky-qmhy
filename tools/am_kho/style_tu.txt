Read as a warm, smiling Mandarin teacher for beginner learners: slow, clear, standard Putonghua (Beijing). Each line is one word or short phrase; read every line exactly once, in order, with a clear pause after each line. Do not skip, repeat, or add anything.
Tones: say each line with a falling statement intonation. Apply tone sandhi: two third tones -> first becomes second (很好 hén hǎo, 很渴 hén kě, 买花 mǎi huā is not sandhi); 一 is yí before 4th tone (一个 yí ge) and yì before 1st/2nd/3rd tone (一家 yì jiā, 一百分 yìbǎi fēn); but 一号楼 yī hào lóu (number one, first tone); 不 is bú before 4th tone (不贵 bú guì, 不记得 bú jìde, 不会 bú huì). In 看一看 the 一 is light neutral yi.
Neutral tones: 的 了 吧 们 子 个(ge) are neutral; 哥哥 gēge, 弟弟 dìdi, 姐姐 jiějie, 告诉 gàosu, 东西 dōngxi (thing), 衣服 yīfu, 喜欢 xǐhuan, 认识 rènshi, 朋友 péngyou, 名字 míngzi, 还是 háishi, 记得 jìde, 觉得 juéde, 关上 guānshang, 后边 hòubian, 家里 jiāli, 过来 guòlai, 回来 huílai, 回去 huíqu, 进来 jìnlai, 进去 jìnqu, 房子 fángzi, 孩子 háizi, 记不住 jì bu zhù.
Special readings: 干 = gān (dry) in 很干 hěn gān, 干衣服 gān yīfu, 干净 gānjìng; 赣 gàn; 干什么 gàn shénme; 还 = hái in 还在 还有 还没 还要 还记得; 还 = huán (give back) in 还书 huán shū, 还钱 huán qián, 还给他 huán gěi tā; 环 huán; 教 = jiāo (teach) in 教汉语 jiāo Hànyǔ, 教孩子 jiāo háizi, 教我写字 jiāo wǒ xiě zì; 教学楼 jiàoxuélóu; 十分 shí fēn (ten minutes); 纷 fēn; 郝 hǎo; 汇 huì; 肩 jiān; 交 jiāo; 好玩 hǎowán; 好喝 hǎohē; 放了学 fàngle xué; 考得很好 kǎo de hěn hǎo; 回答得很好 huídá de hěn hǎo; 看见 kànjiàn; 几个 jǐ ge; 外国 wàiguó; 就是 jiù shì; 觉得不错 juéde búcuò.
